# Product Spec — NextyLeads

## Goal

Replace the NextyLabs marketing workbook with a cleaner operational system without losing any data contained in the workbook.

## Primary user

Single marketing user. Founder uses Reports/Growth as needed.

## Daily flow

Dashboard → Follow-up due → Priority lead → Lead detail → personalized outreach → mark sent → automatic next follow-up.

## Main modules

### Dashboard
- overdue / due today
- Priority A queue
- social verification coverage
- shortcuts to Leads, Research, Templates, Growth

### Leads
- 69 qualified targets
- scores and priority
- research evidence and sources
- social channels
- personalized outreach templates
- status / next action / notes
- activity history

### Follow-up
- D+2 and D+5 tasks created from outbound activity
- overdue / due today / future

### Research
- 129 Prospect Pool
- 88 Research Queue items
- 69 social profiles
- 138 evidence/source rows

### Templates
- common chat templates
- objection handling
- discovery questions
- conversation tree
- offers/pricing guardrails
- mini audit framework
- proposal sections

### Growth
- 30-day content calendar
- content/channel strategy
- founder distribution SOP
- 20 Business Digital Audit campaign
- website/landing plan
- 30/60/90 growth plan
- operating roles
- partnerships / reactivation / referral
- portfolio proof mapping

### Reports
- live CRM funnel
- 30-day Daily KPI plan
- Weekly Review
- Cashflow model

### Settings / Data Vault
- full Excel sync
- 18/18 mapping coverage
- raw workbook snapshot viewer

## Non-goals

- Multi-user RBAC
- AI features
- unofficial WhatsApp bots
- direct cold-message automation without official provider/API compliance
